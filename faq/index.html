About DialX
DialX is a live knowledge event where builders, creatives, and professionals share real experiences and practical ideas from their work, careers, and communities. It is built around the belief that better conversations create better thinking.
DialX Impact 2026 will be held at Redemption City, Nigeria on July 25, 2026.
DialX is for anyone curious about how things work and committed to making them work better — founders, creatives, professionals, students, and builders.
DialX Impact is the first live edition of DialX, focused on how people create real change in their work, communities, and environments through execution and clarity.

Builders Grant
The DialX Impact Builders Grant is a $1,000 cash grant awarded to one builder who is actively building a business, project, or initiative. The winner is announced at DialX Impact 2026 on July 25, 2026.
Anyone actively building a business, project, or initiative can apply. You must have real evidence of progress — not just an idea.
Visit /grantform to complete the application form. Our team will review submissions and contact you directly if you progress to the next stage.
The winner will be announced at DialX Impact 2026 on July 25, 2026.
