About Amos

Behind DialX Africa is Amos Feranmi — a product marketer, community builder, and entrepreneur committed to building platforms that create opportunities and strengthen communities.

While his professional career has been in product marketing, helping technology products reach and serve more people across Africa, his passion has always been building initiatives that create lasting impact within Redemption City through leadership, technology, education, and community.

His work is driven by a simple belief: communities thrive when people have access to the right ideas, meaningful relationships, and opportunities to grow.
