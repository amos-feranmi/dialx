What is DialX

Ideas that
live in the
real world.

DialX is a live knowledge event where people share real experiences and practical ideas that have worked in their work, careers, and communities.

It is built around one belief: ideas only matter when they work in real life.

It exists to turn conversations across Africa into ideas that actually work in real life — shared clearly, honestly, and without the noise.

"Better thinking across Africa comes from better conversations."

The voices on stage.

Builders, thinkers, and practitioners sharing real experience and practical ideas that work.

Chude Jideonwo

Host, #WithChude · Chair, Fourthmainland Creator Fund

Chude Jideonwo

One of Africa's most respected media entrepreneurs and interviewers, Chude Jideonwo has spent over two decades shaping conversations across media, leadership, storytelling, and human flourishing. He is the host of #WithChude, co-founder of RED | For Africa, and founder of Joy, Inc.

Moromoluwatiketike

Content Creator & Producer · Convener, Creators Nexus

Moromoluwatiketike

A leading Christian content creator, actress, producer, and event host, Moromoluwatiketike is known for creating engaging content that inspires faith, purpose, and excellence while building one of Nigeria's growing creative communities.

Oluwafemi Oyewunmi

Founder, School of Wealth NG

Oluwafemi Oyewunmi

Financial educator, pastor, and leadership coach, Oluwafemi Oyewunmi has mentored thousands of individuals through School of Wealth NG, helping people build financial wisdom, strategic thinking, and sustainable wealth.

Oluwatoba Ajayi

Co-founder & CEO, Health Pal

Oluwatoba Ajayi

Product leader, entrepreneur, and community builder, Oluwatoba Ajayi has led initiatives including Christian in Tech, Fashion Fest, Quantum Leap, and Philip Army, while building products that improve access to healthcare across Africa.

Saint Agomeze

Founder, GiNN (Gen Z's Involvement in a New Nigeria)

Saint Agomeze

Entrepreneur, youth advocate, and convener of GiNN, Saint Agomeze is passionate about equipping young Nigerians with the tools, leadership, and opportunities to shape the future through entrepreneurship, innovation, and national development.

Seyi William-Ogunbiyi

Seyi William-Ogunbiyi

A product and operations executive with experience across fintech, e-commerce, and global technology companies including Alibaba, NetEase, GTBank, and Klasha, Seyi brings deep expertise in product strategy, operations, and scaling digital businesses.
