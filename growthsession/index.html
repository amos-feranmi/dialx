Why This Exists

If you're reading this, chances are you applied for the DialX Grant. First — thank you. It was a privilege reading about the businesses being built, the problems being solved, and the ambition behind every application.

While we couldn't support every founder with funding, I wasn't ready for the conversation to end there. After reviewing applications and speaking with founders, I noticed something. The biggest challenge wasn't always funding — it was growth.

  • How do we consistently acquire customers?
  • Which marketing channels should we focus on?
  • Why isn't our product growing faster?
  • What's the right go-to-market strategy?
  • Where should we invest our limited time and budget?

These are the kinds of questions I've spent the last six years helping companies answer. So I'd like to continue supporting a small number of founders through a complimentary Growth Strategy Session — available exclusively to DialX Grant applicants.
