How It Works

Four simple
steps.

01
Record Your Story
Capture a short, authentic video sharing your impact story — in your business, career, community, or daily life.
02
Post on Social
Share your video publicly on Instagram, TikTok, or X (Twitter). Make sure your profile is set to public.
03
Tag @dialxafrica
Tag our official account so we can find and feature your entry. We review every tagged post.
04
Use #DialXImpact
Add the official hashtag to your post. This is how entries are tracked and discovered by the community.
05
Invite as Collaborator
On Instagram, invite @dialxafrica as a collaborator on your post so it appears on our page and reaches more people.
What You Can Win

Prizes worth
showing up for.

The best entries win exciting prizes. Details will be announced closer to the event — follow @dialxafrica to stay updated.

Rules & Guidelines

Play by the
rules.

  1. 01 Videos must be original content created by you — no repurposed or third-party material.
  2. 02 Entries must be publicly visible on your social media profile at the time of judging.
  3. 03 Tag @dialxafrica in your post so we can find and review your submission.
  4. 04 On Instagram, invite @dialxafrica as a collaborator on your post — this is strongly encouraged so the entry reaches our audience.
  5. 05 Use #DialXImpact as a hashtag — this is required for your entry to be counted.
  6. 06 Multiple entries are allowed. Post more than once and give yourself more chances to win.
  7. 07 DialX may feature submitted entries in marketing materials, with credit to the creator.
