How It Worked

Five simple steps.

  1. 01 Record a short video sharing your impact story. Your business, career, community, or everyday life — any real story counts.
  2. 02 Post it publicly on Instagram, TikTok, or X. Your profile must be public at the time of judging.
  3. 03 Tag @dialxafrica in your post. So we can find and review your entry.
  4. 04 Use #DialXImpact as a hashtag. Required for your entry to be counted.
  5. 05 On Instagram, invite @dialxafrica as a collaborator. This gets your entry in front of our audience. Original content only — no repurposed material.
